## :brown[HOW IT WORKS]{align="center"}

Learn to build software by making your own decisions, including the wrong ones.

:brown[[**FORUM**](/forum) & [**ERRATA**](/forum?board=errata)] — ask questions (GENERAL), pitch a project seed (IDEAS), prepare for interviews (INTERVIEWS), or share a mistake and what it taught you (ERRATA). Stories from your own practice are welcome.

:brown[[**READROOM**](/readroom)] — bring code and a question. Write your notes independently. Until the deadline, each reader sees only their own notes. Then everyone's notes become public. A write-up is welcome, not required.

:brown[[**PROJECTS**](/projects)] — take a ticket, explain your approach, and work through review. Ask for help when you get stuck.

Register to join [FORUM](/forum) and [READROOM](/readroom). Sign in later with your username or email. Apply to become a Member when you're ready for project work.
