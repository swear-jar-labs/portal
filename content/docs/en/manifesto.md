## :brown[THE MANIFESTO]{align="center"}

:brown[**Understand what you ship.**] Working code is a start. Know why it works.

:brown[**Make decisions. Revisit them.**] Being wrong is part of learning. Changing your mind is allowed.

:brown[**Review the work. Respect the person.**] Kindness is a rule, not a mood.

:brown[**Leave something useful behind.**] A fix, an explanation, a question worth asking.

:brown[**Tools don't take responsibility. People do.**] Use tools you can judge. Own the result.
