## :brown[THE RULES]{align="center"}

:brown[**Be kind. Be specific.**] Critique the work, not the person. Explain what could be better and why.

:brown[**Understand what you submit.**] You may use AI tools in project work, provided you review all generated code yourself and take responsibility for it. "The model wrote it" is not a review response.

:brown[**Give yourself the practice.**] If you're starting out, write as much as possible yourself, with minimal AI assistance. Make your own decisions and work through the mistakes with more experienced developers.

:brown[**Keep people in the loop.**] If you're stuck or need to step away from a ticket, say so. Asking for help is part of the work.

:brown[**Share responsibly.**] Only post code and materials you have permission to share. Leave passwords, tokens, and other people's private information out.
